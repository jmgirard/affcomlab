Datasets

Seamless Interaction

Over 4,000 hours of face-to-face interaction footage from more than 4,000 participants in diverse contexts, built for social AI research.

Paper Open access

FriendBench

A benchmark for inferring whether two people are friends or strangers from 20-second clips of dyadic conversation: 96 balanced dyads with human ratings and predictions from 26 multimodal models.

Paper Open access

DynAMoS

Dynamic affective movie clips (22 clips) with metadata and ratings of their emotional content from 83 participants, built for subjectivity analysis.

Paper Request access

GFT

Group Formation Task database: 2D video of spontaneous behavior during unscripted social interactions, with frame-level FACS annotations (96 participants).

Paper Request access

MMSE / BP4D+

Multimodal Spontaneous Emotion database: an extension of BP4D with thermal and physiological data and frame-level FACS annotations (140 participants).

Paper Request access

BP4D

Binghamton-Pittsburgh 4D Spontaneous Emotion database: 3D video of spontaneous behavior during emotion elicitation, with frame-level FACS annotations (41 participants).

Paper Request access
